02 / Training

Train for
three rounds.

One permanent weekly programme. No blocks until actual fight camp. Plyometrics and speed come first, then strength, while boxing remains the primary sport-specific work.

Performance standard: round 3 should still have moving legs, fast hands, a functional guard, controlled breathing and clear decisions.

Tuesday / Strength + power

Explosive work while fresh, followed by low-to-moderate volume strength.

Power

Box jumps3 sets × 6 reps
Rotational med-ball throws3 sets × 4 reps/side
Plyometric Push-Ups3 sets × 8 reps

Strength

Trap-bar deadlift3 sets × 3 reps
Bench press3 sets × 5 reps
Bulgarian split squat3 sets × 6 reps/leg
Pull-ups3 sets × 6 reps

Shoulder support

Face pulls2 sets × 15–20 reps
Band/cable external rotations2 sets × 15–20 reps/side

Thursday / Speed + conditioning

The main supplementary conditioning session. Quality output matters more than exhaustion.

Acceleration

15 m accelerations6 reps × 15 m
30 m accelerations4 reps × 30 m

Rest 60–120 sec. Stop chasing speed when mechanics deteriorate.

Boxing-specific conditioning

Rounds4 rounds × 2 min
Rest1 min

Repeat 20 sec high-output punching + 20 sec movement/technical work three times per round. Keep hands active during movement: guard, footwork, defence, feints and resets.

Saturday / Athletic

Strength, power, trunk/hip stability, shoulder endurance and a controlled aerobic base.

Power

Broad jumps3 sets × 4 reps
Explosive push-ups3 sets × 5 reps
Lateral bounds3 sets × 5 reps/side

Strength

Front squat3 sets × 5 reps
Romanian deadlift3 sets × 6 reps
Push press3 sets × 5 reps
Chin-ups3 sets × 6 reps

Core + shoulder

Copenhagen plank3 sets × 20–30 sec/side
Hollow-body hold3 sets × 20–30 sec
Band punches3 sets × 30 sec
Rear-delt raises2 sets × 15–20 reps

Aerobic

30 min

Zone 2. Keep it easy enough to support recovery rather than compromise next week's boxing.

Friday rule: if Friday is hard sparring, reduce Saturday volume. Boxing recovery takes priority.

Progression

Keep the exercises stable and improve execution, load and speed.

Strength

Start around RPE 6–7. Build toward 7–8. When all reps are clean with 1–2 reps in reserve, add a small amount of weight.

Power

Increase jump height, throw quality or sprint quality gradually. Do not add reps just to make the session harder.

Return

First few weeks are re-acclimatisation. Keep loads conservative and let the coach control sparring intensity.
