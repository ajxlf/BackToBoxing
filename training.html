Supplementary training

Block 3 —
Fight Engine.

9 November–6 December. This is the only exercise block included. The objective is to transfer strength and power into repeated boxing-specific output.

Tuesday — Heavy strength + power

Keep strength high while reducing unnecessary volume. Full recovery between explosive efforts.

Strength

Trap-bar deadlift3 × 3
Bench press3 × 4–5
Bulgarian split squat3 × 5 / leg
Pull-ups3 × 5

Power

Box jump3 × 3
Rotational medicine-ball throw4 × 3 / side
Acceleration6 × 20 m

Every explosive repetition should be fast and technically clean. Do not turn power work into conditioning.

Thursday — Fight conditioning

The main conditioning session. The goal is maintaining useful output rather than simply accumulating fatigue.

Speed

Acceleration6 × 15 m
Acceleration4 × 30 m

Full recovery. Sprint quality comes first.

Boxing-specific conditioning

High-output / movement intervals3 × 2 min
Rest1 min

Within each round: 20 sec high output, 20 sec movement/technical work, repeated three times. After the three rounds, recover 3–4 minutes before additional work.

Saturday — Strength + aerobic engine

Maintain force production and build the aerobic system that supports recovery between explosive efforts and rounds.

Strength

Front squat3 × 4
Romanian deadlift3 × 5
Push press3 × 4
Chin-up3 × 5

Athletic + aerobic

Jumps3 × 5
Explosive push-ups3 × 5
Lateral bounds / side3 × 5
Copenhagen + hollow hold3 × 20 sec
Zone 230 min
Friday sparring rule: if Friday is a hard sparring session, reduce Saturday volume rather than forcing the planned session. Boxing quality and recovery take priority.
