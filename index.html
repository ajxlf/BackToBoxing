Block 3 — Fight Engine

9 November–6 December. This is the only training block shown here. The objective is to transfer strength and power into repeated, boxing-specific output.

Keep strength high while reducing unnecessary volume. Full recovery between explosive efforts.

Within each 2-minute round: 20 sec high output, 20 sec movement/technical work, repeated three times. After the three rounds, recover 3–4 minutes before any additional work.

Mobility

Daily 10-minute reset

You're very tight and have never been able to touch your toes. Consistency matters more than forcing range.

Lower body

Ankle dorsiflexion2 × 10/side
90/90 hip rotations2 × 8/side
Adductor rock-backs2 × 10
Couch stretch2 × 30 sec/side

Posterior chain

Hamstring stretch2 × 30 sec/side
Deep squat hold2 × 30 sec

Use controlled breathing. Never force a painful stretch.

Upper body

Thoracic rotations2 × 8/side

After training, longer static holds of 30–60 seconds can be used for calves, hamstrings, hip flexors, adductors, glutes, thoracic spine and shoulders.

Monitoring

Measure what matters.

The aim is not to become lighter at the expense of performance. The key question is whether round three still looks like you.

Every morning

Every 4 weeks

  • 7-day weight average
  • Waist measurement
  • Trap-bar / squat / bench / pull-up performance
  • Broad jump
  • 10–20 m sprint
  • 3 × 2 min controlled bag test
The real conditioning test: by December, complete 3 × 2-minute rounds with 1-minute rests while keeping your legs moving, hands fast, shoulders usable, breathing controlled and decision-making intact. Conditioning is successful when round 3 does not collapse compared with round 1.

Non-negotiables

Don't crash diet.

You need fuel to train and preserve strength.

Don't chase exhaustion.

Sprints and power work need quality and recovery.

Don't dehydrate to make weight.

The goal is to live around fight weight.

Protect Sunday.

Recovery is part of the programme.

Respect the coach.

If sparring load rises, supplementary volume comes down.

Strength stays.

Maintain force production throughout the cut.
