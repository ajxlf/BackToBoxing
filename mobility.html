Mobility

Move better.

Daily mobility is emphasised because you are very tight and have never been able to touch your toes. Consistency matters more than forcing range.

Daily

10-minute reset.

Controlled range, relaxed breathing and no forcing through pain.

Lower body

Ankle dorsiflexion2 × 10/side
90/90 hip rotations2 × 8/side
Adductor rock-backs2 × 10
Couch stretch2 × 30 sec/side

Posterior chain

Hamstring stretch2 × 30 sec/side
Deep squat hold2 × 30 sec

Upper body

Thoracic rotations2 × 8/side

After training, use 30–60 sec static holds for calves, hamstrings, hip flexors, adductors, glutes, thoracic spine and shoulders.

Why it is here

Better usable range supports footwork, stance, rotation and general movement quality. Mobility work should complement boxing rather than leave you fatigued.

If flexibility does not improve: because you have never been able to touch your toes despite repeated stretching, consider a physiotherapy assessment rather than simply forcing deeper stretches.
