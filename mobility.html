05 / Mobility

Move
better.

A short daily routine for usable range in stance, footwork and rotation. The aim is controlled movement, not forcing flexibility.

Daily / 10 minutes

Move slowly, breathe normally and stay within a controlled, non-painful range.

Lower body

Ankle dorsiflexion2 × 10/side
90/90 hip rotations2 × 8/side
Adductor rock-backs2 × 10
Couch stretch2 × 30 sec/side

Posterior chain + squat

Hamstring stretch2 × 30 sec/side
Deep squat hold2 × 30 sec

Upper body

Thoracic rotations2 × 8/side

Long-term flexibility

You have always been very tight and have never been able to touch your toes. Treat this as a long-term project: consistency, controlled range and gradual exposure.

If range never improves: if consistent mobility work produces no meaningful change, consider a physiotherapy assessment rather than repeatedly forcing deeper stretches.
