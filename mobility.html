Mobility + flexibility

Move with
less restriction.

A short daily routine is easier to sustain than occasional long stretching sessions. The goal is usable range for stance, footwork, rotation and general athletic movement—not forcing yourself into positions.

Daily

10-minute reset.

Move slowly, breathe normally and stay within a controlled, non-painful range.

Lower body

Ankle dorsiflexion2 × 10/side
90/90 hip rotations2 × 8/side
Adductor rock-backs2 × 10
Couch stretch2 × 30 sec/side

Posterior chain + squat

Hamstring stretch2 × 30 sec/side
Deep squat hold2 × 30 sec

Upper body

Thoracic rotations2 × 8/side

Thoracic mobility supports rotation and helps you access boxing positions without trying to create movement by forcing the lower back.

Flexibility priority

You have always been very tight and have never been able to touch your toes. Treat this as a long-term mobility project. Consistency, controlled range and gradual exposure matter more than forcing the end position.

If range never improves: if consistent mobility work produces no meaningful change, consider a physiotherapy assessment rather than repeatedly forcing deeper stretches.
