Creatine

Creatine is available but is not being introduced until the initial weight trend is established, because it can increase scale weight through water retention and make the early trend harder to interpret.

Food preferences

Core proteins: chicken, eggs, tuna and salmon. Fruit and avocado are excluded. Cucumber is excluded. Two meals is preferred, but three meals are fine when training demands it.
