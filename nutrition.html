Rules

Consistency matters more than perfect individual days.

Weight trend

Use morning weigh-ins after the toilet and before food or drink. Judge the seven-day average, not a single reading.

Carbohydrate

Do not eliminate carbs simply because the scale sometimes moves faster when they are removed; glycogen and associated water can change scale weight quickly.

Creatine

Delay introduction until the initial weight trend is established if you want a cleaner baseline, because creatine can increase scale weight through water retention.

Sunday prep

Bulk-cook chicken, lean mince, rice, potatoes and vegetables. Portion meals so weekday decisions are minimal.
